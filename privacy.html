Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Effective date / Yürürlük tarihi: 9 November 2026

Zombirium for iOS

Publisher: Emre Tok. This policy covers the iOS version of Zombirium.

Game data

The game works without an account. Campaign progress and settings are stored locally on your device. This iOS release does not include an active advertising or analytics SDK, does not track users, and does not automatically transmit personal data to the publisher. Motion sensor input used for visual effects is processed on the device.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Email is handled by the email providers involved. We do not sell support information. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing emre1234tok@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can stop playing at any time. Removing the app removes its local app data; device backups are managed by your device and Apple settings. Apple may process store, device or diagnostic information under its own privacy policy and your device preferences.

Contact and updates

Questions: emre1234tok@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
